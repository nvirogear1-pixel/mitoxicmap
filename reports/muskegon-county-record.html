What this page is, and what it is not

It is a rendering of two state data layers and one federal inventory, queried on the date below. Each figure is a field value or a count of field values. Nothing is scored, ranked or interpreted.

It is not a site assessment, a Phase I or II, a health risk evaluation, or a substitute for testing. Points are record locations, not plume boundaries. A record absent from these layers is not proof that ground is clean, and a record present is not proof that a specific parcel is affected.
