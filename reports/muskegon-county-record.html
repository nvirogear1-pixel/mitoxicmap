What this page is, and what it is not

This page is a map of published records. Every count above is a record count, not a count of contaminated properties. One property can carry several records; a record can exist at a site that has been cleaned up; a site that was never investigated will not appear at all.
It is not an assessment of any property. Locations are published points, not plume boundaries. A missing record is not evidence that ground is clean. The register does not measure anyone's well, and it does not say who is exposed.
