READ ME - provenance and method

Geometry provenance: every boundary is the county's own polygon, pulled live 2026-10-21 from Muskegon County's Land_Bank_Properties MapServer (Status FS = For Sale). No approximations, no AI-generated shapes.

Acreage method: calculated from the county ring via the shoelace method (WGS84 scaled at local latitude), stored side-by-side with county-reported values. Disagreements flagged, never silently chosen.

Counts reconciled: 368 total parcels carry Land Bank ownership in the county layer; 289 carry FS status (shown here). Earlier counts of 243 and 289 were status-filter variance - the drift flag is closed with this explanation.

Point vs polygon vs platted lot: a point marker is an approximate dot; a parcel polygon is the county's legal tax-parcel boundary; a platted lot is a historical subdivision lot.
