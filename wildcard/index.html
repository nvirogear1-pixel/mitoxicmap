What this is. Every price came from a live listing feed. Every parcel number, acreage and owner type came from Muskegon County GIS. Every environmental line was run against EGLE's Part 201 register on November 8, 2026, and states what was found, how close, what the contaminants are, and whether the screen ran at the parcel or at an approximate location.

What it is not. Not an inspection, not a condition opinion, not flood certification, not title work. Interior condition is unknown on every listing. Where two sources disagreed on a price, the page says so instead of picking one.
