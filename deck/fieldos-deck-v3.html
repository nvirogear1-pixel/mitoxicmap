Platform Architecture

The Five Pillars of FieldOS

01

Frictionless Customer Intake

Zero account creation. Customer drops pins on a satellite map, marks every asset, uploads photos, and submits. Contractor receives a complete digital job package before leaving the office.

Outcome: Faster estimate turnaround, zero office-centric friction, substantially higher lead conversion.

02

Property-Centric Digital Twin

Data organized around physical assets and geospatial pins — not just text-based customer names. GPS location, hazard ratings, measurements, crew notes, before/after photos, final invoices.

Outcome: Permanent historical record. Eliminates crew confusion, standardizes institutional knowledge.

03

Permanent Chain-of-Evidence

Integrates evidentiary documentation into the core workflow: original request → pre-work photos → hazards & scope → completion photos → invoice history.

Outcome: Dramatically reduces legal exposure, minimizes conflict resolution time, expedites payment.

04

Automated Safety Compliance Engine

Embedded daily safety checks: chainsaw inspections, crane & bucket truck checks, PPE verification, power-line assessments, storm hazard evaluations.

Outcome: Lowers insurance premiums, ensures enterprise-grade accountability, provides instant auditability.

05

AI-Generated Marketing Flywheel

Job completed → Before/After photos uploaded → AI drafts content (Facebook, IG, Google Business) → Owner 1-click approves → Published.

Outcome: Reduces marketing labor costs, creates a self-sustaining advertising engine from operational data.
