The Industry Is Still Running on Paper

❌ How It Works Today

Tree care contractors manage their businesses with a combination of phone calls, spreadsheets, paper work orders, and consumer apps never designed for field operations.

Contractors spend 2–4 hours driving to properties just to provide an estimate — before a single dollar is earned.
