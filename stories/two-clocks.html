Heights-Ravenna Road — Muskegon County, MI

Two Clocks Running

One clock tracks when the chemicals arrived. The other tracks when anyone was allowed to know what they do.

The gap between these two clocks is measured in decades. The people who live on Heights-Ravenna Road were never told what was in the ground beneath their homes. This is the timeline — documented, sourced, and preserved.

Doctrine: No causal claims. No before/after disease comparison. The absence of a baseline IS the finding.

1984 — The Cancer Registry

Michigan establishes its first systematic cancer tracking. 37 years after PFAS entered commerce.

Act 82 of 1984 — effective July 1, 1984 (MCL 333.2619). Michigan's cancer registry begins. But it records where cancer happens, not what caused it. And it does not cover the decades before 1984.

For residents of Heights-Ravenna Road, there is no baseline. No one was counting before 1984.

The absence of a baseline IS the finding. We are not claiming causation. We are documenting that no baseline existed against which to measure.

VERIFIED Source: Michigan Legislature, MCL 333.2619

1961 — What They Knew

Internal industry animal studies showing PFAS toxicity begin. Not disclosed publicly for decades.

Documented through court records: Internal industry studies showing PFAS health concerns date to the early 1960s. This information was established in litigation discovery — not voluntary disclosure. The manufacturers' Knowledge Clock started decades before the public's Knowledge Clock.

The people on Heights-Ravenna Road did not have access to this information when they bought their homes, raised their children, or drank their water.

DOCUMENTED Source: Court records, litigation discovery

2005-2013 — The C8 Science Panel

69,000 people studied. April 2012: probable link to kidney cancer, testicular cancer, thyroid disease confirmed.

C8 Science Panel (2005-2013): Epidemiological study of ~69,000 people exposed to PFOA through contaminated drinking water. April 2012: probable link published between PFOA exposure and six health conditions including kidney cancer, testicular cancer, and thyroid disease.

65 years after PFAS entered commerce, the scientific proof arrives. For Heights-Ravenna residents, it arrives as news.

VERIFIED Source: C8 Science Panel publications, EPA

2021 — The Building Goes Up

New construction at 3523. BEA filed same year. Contamination stays in the ground.

2021: New building completed at 3523 Heights-Ravenna Rd. Same year: Baseline Environmental Assessment filed by H&A Property Investment Group LLC. The BEA shields the new owner from cleanup liability. The contamination stays. The building goes up above it.
The neighbors were not notified. There is no legal requirement to notify them. That is not an oversight. That is the system working as designed.

VERIFIED Source: EGLE BEA Records + Muskegon County GIS

The Doctrine

What we do not say. What we do say. Why the distinction matters.

What we do NOT say: PFAS caused any specific person's illness. The laundromat is responsible for every health issue. Before/after disease comparison (no baseline exists).
What we DO say: The timeline is documented. The contamination is confirmed. The residents were not notified. The tax code has an unused environmental adjustment mechanism. The evidence is hashed and anchored.
The path forward: The Community Exposure Registry creates the dataset. The dataset justifies the study. The study forces accountability. Not accusation — documentation.
