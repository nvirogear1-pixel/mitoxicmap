Before You Begin — Please Read & Acknowledge

This is a voluntary community registry. You are not required to participate. You are not required to provide your name, address, or contact information. You may submit anonymously with only your general area and health observations.

Your information will be kept confidential. Nvirogear does not sell, share, or publish individual identifying information. Aggregated data may be used to advocate for health studies, but individual entries remain private unless you explicitly give written permission.

This is not medical advice. Nvirogear does not diagnose illness, assign causation, or guarantee any outcome. If you have a health concern, contact your doctor. If you have an environmental complaint, contact EGLE at 800-662-9278.
