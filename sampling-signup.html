What Is Community Sampling?

Nvirogear is working to bring schools, community centers, and certified labs together to offer PFAS water testing to residents who may be exposed.

  • We help organize sampling events in your area
  • Water samples are collected by community members with guidance
  • Samples are sent to a certified lab for analysis
  • Results are mapped on MITOXICMAP — free and public
  • This is community science — not a government program — but the data is real and admissible
