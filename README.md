# MITOXICMAP.info

Michigan's environmental contamination story — told through interactive maps, community health data, and documented evidence.

## What's Here

- **Interactive Story Maps** — Guided tours through three contamination sites: BASF Wyandotte (Detroit), Heights-Ravenna (Muskegon), and Cadillac (Wexford County)
- **Contamination Health Study Registry** — Community self-reporting tool for residents anywhere in Michigan
- **Water Sampling Sign-Up** — Community-led PFAS testing coordination
- **Investment Deck** — Nvirogear TreeOS pitch deck with real production data

## Stories

1. **BASF Wyandotte** — 230-acre chemical plant discharging 60 gpm toxic groundwater into the Detroit River
2. **Heights-Ravenna** — PFAS contamination from former laundromat, PFOS 221 ppt (14× legal limit)
3. **Cadillac** — Northernaire cyanide, KYSOR Superfund, Velsicol PBBs, and ongoing PFAS plumes

## Key Findings

- $92.8M in grants across three sites — 0% allocated to industrial source stoppage
- 18,885 EGLE Part 201 contamination sites statewide (this project covers 3)
- Community exposure registry built — dataset justifies the study

## Tech

- Static HTML/CSS/JS — no build step, no database required
- Leaflet.js for interactive maps
- Deployed on Netlify at mitoxicmap.info
